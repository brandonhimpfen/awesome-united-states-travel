## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, ESTA applications, and weather tracking.

- [Brand USA (Official Travel Portal - Visit The USA)](https://www.visittheusa.com/) - Official national tourism board guide featuring destination highlights, cross-country road trips, and regional guides.
- [U.S. Customs and Border Protection - ESTA Portal](https://esta.cbp.dhs.gov/) - Official U.S. Government portal for applying for electronic travel authorization under the Visa Waiver Program.
- [National Weather Service (NWS)](https://www.weather.gov/) - Official national meteorological agency providing real-time forecasts, severe weather warnings, and climate radar tracking.

## Transportation & Rail

Navigating national passenger rail networks, intercity buses, domestic aviation, and transit systems.

- [Amtrak (National Railroad Passenger Corporation)](https://www.amtrak.com/) - Official national railway operator managing intercity passenger rail lines, including Acela high-speed service in the Northeast Corridor and scenic long-distance routes.
- [Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)](https://www.faa.gov/) - Official aviation authority managing national airspace, airport delays, and flight safety information.
- [Greyhound Lines](https://www.greyhound.com/) - Major long-distance intercity coach network connecting cities and towns across the United States.

## Destinations & Regions

### New York & Northeast

- [NYC Tourism + Conventions (Official City Portal)](https://www.nyctourism.com/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the five boroughs, Broadway, museums, and public transit.
- [I Love NY (New York State Tourism)](https://www.iloveny.com/) - Official regional portal for discovering Hudson Valley historic sites, the Adirondacks, and Finger Lakes wine country.

### California & West Coast

- [Visit California (Official State Portal)](https://www.visitcalifornia.com/) - Official state travel guide for exploring San Francisco, Los Angeles, national parks, and Pacific Coast Highway drives.

### Florida & Southeast

- [VISIT FLORIDA (Official State Portal)](https://www.visitflorida.com/) - Official state travel guide for exploring Miami, Orlando theme parks, the Florida Keys, and coastal beaches.

### National Parks & Southwest

- [Visit Arizona (Grand Canyon State Portal)](https://www.visitarizona.com/) - Official regional travel guide for exploring the Grand Canyon, Sedona red rocks, and desert national monuments.

## National Parks & Conservation

Resources for exploring America's protected federal public lands, monuments, and recreational areas.

- [National Park Service (NPS Official Portal)](https://www.nps.gov/) - Official federal agency managing America's 400+ national parks, monuments, historical sites, and recreation areas.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contact numbers, medical assistance, and traveler safety protocols.

- [Emergency Services (Police, Fire, Ambulance - Dial 911)](https://www.usa.gov/) - Direct nationwide emergency dispatch number operational across all states and territories.
